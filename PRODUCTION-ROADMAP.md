## 3. A few words explained (very simply)

### 🔤 WABA — "WhatsApp Business Account"
- **Full form:** **W**hatsApp **B**usiness **A**ccount.
- **What it is:** Think of it as Interval's **"business folder" inside WhatsApp**.
  It holds our business name, our phone number(s), and our message history rules.
- You don't touch it every day — it's just the official container that says
  "this WhatsApp belongs to Interval."

### ⏰ The "24-Hour Window"
This is the single most important rule to understand about cost and messaging.

- When a **customer messages us first**, a **24-hour timer** starts.
- **Inside those 24 hours:** we can reply with **any normal message we want, freely**
  (like a real chat) — and it is **FREE**.
- **After 24 hours pass** with no reply from the customer, the "free chat door"
  closes. To message them again, we can no longer send a plain message — we must
  send a **template** (explained next).

> Simple picture: A customer knocks on our door (messages us). The door stays open
> for **24 hours** — during that time we chat freely and for free. After 24 hours the
> door shuts, and to knock on *their* door we need a special pre-approved note
> (a template).

### 📝 Template — "a pre-approved message"
- **What it is:** A message that we **write in advance and Meta approves once**.
  Examples: *"Hi {name}, your class is tomorrow at 5 PM"* or *"Your fee payment of
  ₹{amount} is received."*
- **Why it exists:** To stop businesses from spamming people. Meta checks the message
  once, and after that we're allowed to send it.
- **When we need it:** Only when **we** start the conversation, or when we reply
  **after** the 24-hour window has closed.
- **When we do NOT need it:** When a customer messaged us first and we reply within
  24 hours — normal free chat, no template needed.

## 5. What does it cost? (very simple)

WhatsApp is **pay-as-you-go** — there is **no monthly fee**. You only pay for certain
messages.

| Situation | Cost |
|-----------|------|
| Customer messages us first, and we reply within **24 hours** | **FREE** ✅ |
| We send a **template** (we start the chat, or reply after 24 hrs) | **Small paid fee per message** 💰 |

The paid templates come in three types (cheapest to most expensive in India):

1. **Utility** — helpful updates like *"Your fee is received"* → very cheap
   *(and even FREE if sent while the 24-hour window is still open)*.
2. **Authentication** — one-time passwords / login codes → cheap.
3. **Marketing** — offers and promotions → the most expensive.

> **Bottom line for Interval:** Answering students and parents is **free**. Sending
> reminders and OTPs costs a **tiny amount each**. Big promotional blasts cost the
> most. India rates are among the lowest in the world, so day-to-day cost is small.
